What do these mean?

Std dev = how bumpy the ride is · Beta = how much it swings versus the benchmark · Alpha = whether it beat what its risk level predicts · Sharpe = reward earned per unit of risk · R2 = how much of that swing the benchmark actually explains (low R2 means treat beta/alpha with caution).
